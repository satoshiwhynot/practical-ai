# One-person content studio

**Template kit.** Turn your expertise, notes or an approved interview into a useful piece of content.

1. Answer the positioning worksheet in [WORKING-KIT.md](WORKING-KIT.md).
2. Gather source notes and a writing sample.
3. Use **Prepare the source brief** before asking for a draft.
4. Draft one piece that answers one reader question.
5. Review it using the publishing checklist and record what the work taught you.

The working kit includes positioning questions, source interviews, five prompts, a delivery-economics calculation and a first-month plan. Choose your own audience or client work as the primary route.

Keep factual claims attached to sources. Financial inputs are hypothetical illustrations, not rates or earnings claims.
